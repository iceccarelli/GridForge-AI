Recommendation

Full DLC plus behind-the-meter supply

The same hall with on-site firm supply where the grid connection binds. The only scenario whose capacity is set by what can be built rather than by the queue.

  • Get the DSO position in writing on firm capacity and any flexibility product. It costs nothing but time and it can end the question.
  • Commission a structural assessment of the floor and a hydraulic model of the secondary loop. Both are cheap relative to the decision and both can invalidate it.
  • Place long-lead orders only after those clear — lead time, not construction, governs the programme.
  • Instrument the hall and reconcile this model against twelve months of measured data before phase two. That is what moves these numbers from estimated to field-validated.

Boundary

What this is, and what it is not

  • An engineering opinion supported by a documented model. Not a design package, and it confers no design liability.
  • No stamped drawings, no CFD of this hall, no equipment selection or bill of materials.
  • The floor-loading check is a screening calculation; a structural engineer must sign it off.
  • We quote no equipment and take no margin on hardware. Nothing here routes you to a supplier we are paid by.

Nothing in this deck is a measurement of this asset unless it is labelled E5 or above.
