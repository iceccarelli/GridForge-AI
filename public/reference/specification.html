4. How responses will be evaluated

Price is one criterion of five. The item being bought exists to unlock compute on a date: a response that is cheaper and materially slower is the expensive one, and the weighting says so.

C1Compliance with the mandatory duty35%Every 'shall' requirement met, at the site conditions stated. A duty quoted at a reference condition rather than at ours scores nothing.
C2Delivered programme30%Weeks to site and to energisation, and which long-lead item governs. Assessed against what the item does to the energisation date.
C3Price20%Delivered capex, and price per kW actually unlocked rather than price per unit of equipment.

A response that does not meet every 'shall' requirement is not ranked above one that does, whatever its price. That ordering is the entire point of a mandatory requirement.
